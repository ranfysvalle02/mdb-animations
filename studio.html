              How Inkling works

              Draw it once. Animate it anywhere.

              AI drafts. You decide. Every stroke is remembered: who drew it, how, and where it’s used.

              1. ✏️ Draw it onceFinger, pencil, or mouse; save it as an asset. Grow lookalikes from your strokes.“Red Rocket”, plus 9 for a battle
              2. 🎞 Animate itPlace it in a scene, add frames, slide or redraw what moves. Tweak any frame.The rocket lifts off in 6 frames.
              3. 🔍 Find anythingAssets, scenes, or single frames: describe it, sketch it, or pick an asset.“the moment it clears the trees”
              4. 🧬 Always knowWhere it's used, what's outdated, what looks like it, and how it was made, stroke by stroke.“Which frames still show the old rocket?” · watch Little Fox go from doodle to v3
