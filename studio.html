              Under the hood

              Every stroke has an author.

              AI as studio scaffolding, not a slot machine. Here is every moving part, in plain words.

              1. ✏️You drawpressure, order, timing
              2. {}Strokeslines, not pixels
              3. 🧭Voyage AIa map of meaning
              4. 🍃MongoDB Atlasyour private shelf
              5. ◎Find similarin milliseconds
              1. 🎞 Choreography, not replacementGemini picks a few poses. Plain math moves your actual strokes between them. Your line never goes through a filter, so it never flickers or drifts off-model.
              2. ✍ Every stroke has an authorAI drafts stay blue-pencil previews until you say yes, and stay marked after. The meter on the canvas shows how much is your hand.
              3. ◎ Your sketchbook, rememberedDraw a rough shape to find matching work. Voyage AI maps meaning; Atlas searches only your library. No folders called final_v3_REAL.

              ✏️ Your drawing is data, not a photo

              Every line is saved as the path your hand took: where it went, how hard you pressed, and in what order. That is why Inkling can replay your process, copy and paste, turn art into a brush, and pose your own strokes into a flipbook. It is all math on your lines.

              🧭 Voyage AI: a map of meaning
              
          
              Voyage’s multimodal model (voyage-multimodal-3.5) turns a picture or a sentence into a list of numbers: coordinates on one shared map. Things that look or mean alike land close together, so your fox doodle and the words “a cute animal” are neighbors. That is how Find similar works without you ever tagging anything.
              
          
              Saving or searching sends that drawing or text to Voyage to compute its coordinates.
              
        
              
        
      
              

      
              
        
              
          
              🍃 MongoDB Atlas: your private shelf
              
          
              Every drawing, version, flipbook frame, video, and its coordinates live together in one database. Atlas Vector Search finds the nearest neighbors on the map, filtered to your library only, so one artist’s search can never see another’s art. Ask “the moment the rocket leaves the ground” and it can land on the exact frame.

              The Inkling API
          
              
            
              strokes
              Your drawing, in the order you drew it: {points: [{x, y, pressure}], color, width, layer, order}. The paper is W × H (1000 × 650).
              
            
              frames, layers
              Every flipbook frame (each a list of strokes), and frame 1 by layer.
              
            
              ink(points, {color, width, fill})
              Draws a stroke and records it for baking. Points can have a z in 3D.
              
            
              inkStroke(stroke, map)
              Inks one of your strokes, moving each point with map(p, i).
              
            
              loopT(), LOOP
              0–1 through the loop that gets baked. Drive motion with it for a seamless flipbook.
              
            
              KEEP_DRAWING = true
              Keeps your drawing visible under the baked layer.
              
          
              
          
              Plain p5.js, Processing for the web, in a sandbox with no network. random() and noise() are seeded, so a bake is repeatable.
